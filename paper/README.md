# Paper draft

`zzn.tex` describes the Lean proof in [`../proof/`](../proof/): the catalogue, its soundness and
completeness, the decision procedure, the construction `solveZ3 (leafL thinSolveW2 tabAll)`, and
the step bounds. The Lean names it cites are listed with their places in
[`../docs/PROOF.md`](../docs/PROOF.md).

`supplement.tex` holds the material the paper points to by section title: the plug DP encoding,
the separation fact, certificate formats, the entry-by-entry argument for pair deletions,
pseudocode for block selection and the splices, the step accounting and the map from the paper's
statements to Lean names.

Figures are generated by `figures/src/*.py` and `../docs/figures/make_figures.py` (SVG, converted
to PDF with Inkscape).

The constants of the step-count section (K(10), K(16), the expanded bound) are evaluated by
`tools/kwr.py`, a transcription of the Lean definitions; Lean evaluates them only at b = 2, 3.

Build with `make` (outputs `build/zzn.pdf` and `build/supplement.pdf`). Needs TeX Live with
`texlive-latex-extra` and `texlive-science` (for `algorithm` and `algpseudocode`).
